Privacy Policy — Poker Chips

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Poker Chips is a chip counter for poker games played in person. This policy explains what the app handles, why, and for how long. It is written to be read; if anything is unclear, get in touch.

Data controller: Dídac Clària. Contact: didacclaria99@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There is no sign-up. We never ask for an email, a password, or access to your contacts, photos or location.
  • One-phone mode sends nothing. Play that way and the game never leaves the device; only the advertising section below applies to you.
  • Data is stored in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Everything deletes itself after 7 days without activity: the table, the name you typed, and the anonymous account itself.
  • And you can delete it yourself now, from the app, without emailing anybody.

1. What we handle

Playing with several phones (online mode)

Data Why
An anonymous identifier, created the first time you open or join a table So your seat is waiting when you come back, and so only you can act on your turn
The name you type when you sit down So everyone knows whose chips are whose. You choose it, and it can be anything
The game: chips, bets, hands played, rebuys and timestamps This is the service itself — keeping the table's books

The anonymous identifier is not tied to any personal detail about you: no email, no phone number, and no real name beyond whatever you type.

The name is not stored separately: it lives inside the game, so it goes when the game does. We keep no profile of you.

Playing with one phone

Nothing. The game is stored on the device and is never sent anywhere.

Advertising

The app shows at most one ad, when a game ends, through Google AdMob. Google may process the device's advertising identifier and technical data under its own policy.

On iOS we ask first, through Apple's tracking transparency prompt. Decline and you will still see an ad, but not a personalised one.

What we never handle

Location, contacts, photos, microphone, files, browsing history, health data or financial data. The camera is used only to scan a table's QR code, and no image is stored or transmitted.

2. Where it is stored

With Supabase, on a database hosted in the European Union (Ireland). Advertising data is processed by Google under its own policy.

3. How long it is kept

  • Tables are deleted automatically after 7 days without activity, and the name you typed goes with them. That is not an intention — a job does it nightly.
  • Anonymous accounts left with no tables at all are deleted too. An identity with nothing behind it is no use to anybody.
  • The game saved on your phone goes when you finish it or uninstall the app.

4. Who it is shared with

Nobody, except:

  • The other players at your table, who see the name you typed and your chip stack. That is what sitting down means.
  • Supabase, as the hosting provider.
  • Google, for advertising.

We do not sell data. There is no third-party analytics and no social network integration.

5. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Performance of the service you asked for: the table, your seat and the accounting.
  • Consent for personalised advertising, which you can withdraw at any time in your device settings.
  • Legitimate interest in showing non-personalised advertising to sustain a free app.

6. Your rights

You can delete everything yourself, right now, without asking: on the home screen, "Privacy and data" → "Delete my data". Your account, your name, your seats and any tables you opened all go. It is immediate and irreversible.

For anything else — access, rectification, restriction, portability or objection — write to didacclaria99@gmail.com. We answer within one month.

Because the account is anonymous, to find your data we will need the table code you played at and the name you used.

If you believe we are handling your data improperly, you can complain to your national data protection authority — in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Poker Chips is not gambling: no real money can be staked, there are no prizes or rewards, and the chips have no monetary value.

8. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above, and if the change matters we will say so in the app.
